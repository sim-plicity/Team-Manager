# Sim-plicity Team Manager

Sim-plicity Team Manager is a tool designed to help you configure, monitor, and manage your direct-drive wheelbase. Whether you are using current Sim-plicity hardware or a legacy OSW (Open Sim Wheel) setup, Team Manager provides a single, unified interface to get the most out of your gear.

## Why we built Team Manager

At Sim-plicity, we believe in supporting hardware for the long haul. Since we began manufacturing in 2015, our goal has been to provide reliable, high-quality gear that remains capable for years to come.

Team Manager is our way of providing that continuity. It bridges the gap between older, community-supported hardware and our current Gen 2 and Gen 3 integrated platforms. We believe in platform continuity, ensuring you have the right tools to master your experience regardless of which generation you are running.

## Key Features

We've focused on the features that matter most to your daily driving experience:

* **Per-game FFB profiles:** Easily save and load specific settings for every title in your library so your wheel feels right every time you jump into a new race.
* **Live FFB Gain Mixer:** Adjust your gain and effects in real-time without having to exit your sim or disconnect your hardware.
* **Firmware Management:** A simple way to view, upgrade, or downgrade your firmware to keep your hardware running exactly how you want it.
* **Automatic Detection:** The app automatically recognizes your installed sim racing titles and connected hardware to save you time.
* **Hardware Diagnostics:** Built-in utilities for testing & troubleshooting.
* **Seamless Updates:** The app features a built-in self-updater for easy, single-stage updates directly from within the application.

Team Manager supports a wide range of hardware across multiple generations.

## Technical Essentials

### System Requirements
* **OS:** Windows 10 x64 or later
* **Runtime:** .NET 8.0 (included in the installer)
* **Connection:** USB 2.0+ port
* **Internet:** Required for firmware downloads and account features (offline use is supported)

### Installation & Updates

**New Installations (TM_Installer):**
For a new PC, we recommend using the **TM_Installer**. This tool performs a multi-stage update to bring your system from the baseline (v25.03.03) directly to the latest version in one click.

**[Download TM_Installer](https://github.com/sim-plicity/Team-Manager/releases/download/v25.03.03/TM_Installer.exe)**

**Ongoing Updates (In-App):**
Once installed, Team Manager handles updates via its built-in self-updater. The app checks for updates on startup and provides a simple, single-click process to move to the next required version.
